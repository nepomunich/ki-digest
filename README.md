# Daily AI in news email digest

## Overview

I am a project manager dealing with AI in journalism. I want you to write me a email digest from a couple of web sources every morning at 8 a.m. to XXX  

## Sources

- the AI folder in my Inoreader account
- different newsletters from my inbox 
- different LinkedIn newsletters

## Contents

- The daily mail should be structured as follows:
- Section on new AI prodcuts, use cases or projects in journalism if applicable
- Section on major AI news like new models, regulation, ethical implications, AI infrastructure from the US
- Section on major AI news like new models, regulation, ethical implications, AI infrastructure from Europe
- Updates on international conferences focussing on AI in journalism if applicable
- Suggestion for new website, blog or social account that is worth to follow 

- There should be now more than three entries per section in a single mail

## Criteria for selection

- newsworthyness
- relevance for my work as project manager dealing with AI in journalism. I am particularly interested in how media adapt to AI and make use of AI in new products. 
- 

## Styleguide
- Write precisely 
- Explain the how in editorial ai use cases
- In AI news focus focus on the questions who what and how
- Write no more than four sentences per entry
- Always include the link to the original source
